    07 / COLLECTOR FAQ

    Questions about worldwide adoption.

    How are fragile clay dioramas protected during international shipping? +

    Every sculpture is sealed with a protective matte finish, cushioned in multi-layer soft foam inside a sturdy inner box, and double-boxed for international transit from Vietnam with full tracking.

    How do the built-in fairy lights and lanterns work? +

    Each illuminated diorama features hidden warm-white LED wiring connected to a discreet, easy-to-reach toggle switch and replaceable coin/button battery pack—no messy cords on your desk.

    Can I request a custom pet sculpture or golden name plaque? +

    Yes! You can add a hand-lettered golden name on the back of any shrine (like our “Mr. Karren” piece) or inquire about a custom pet memorial or fantasy scene via our custom request form or Etsy messages.

    Since pieces are 1-of-1, what if my favorite diorama is already adopted? +

    Join our Early Access Waitlist above or message Giang on Etsy. While every piece has its own unique charm, we occasionally open limited made-to-order slots for beloved themes.
